# Trademark policy

The GPL covers the code. It does not cover the name.

"PhonePad" and "DynShift", and the PhonePad logo, are unregistered trademarks
(™) of the PhonePad maintainer, trading as DynShift. Rights in an unregistered mark arise from use, and this
document is a statement of that use. No registration has been filed.

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your software works with PhonePad, is compatible with PhonePad, or
  is built on PhonePad.
- Use the name in truthful writing about the project: articles, reviews,
  tutorials, comparisons, bug reports, academic work.
- Redistribute PhonePad unmodified, under the GPL, with its name intact.
- Use the name in a repository description such as "a fork of PhonePad".

## What needs a different name

If you modify PhonePad and distribute it, **rename it**. The GPL gives you the
right to fork the code; it does not give you the right to call your fork
PhonePad, and a user downloading something called PhonePad should get this
project.

Concretely, a modified distribution must not:

- be named PhonePad, or a name likely to be mistaken for it
  (PhonePad Pro, PhonePad+, PhonePadX, PhonePad Plus, and so on);
- use the PhonePad logo or its visual identity as its own;
- publish under a domain, app-store listing or account name that implies it is
  the official project.

This is the ordinary arrangement for copyleft projects — Firefox, Rust and
Chromium all separate code licence from name in the same way. It exists so that
"PhonePad" keeps meaning one thing, not to restrict what you can build.

## The logo and brand assets

The logo, wordmark and site design are **not** covered by the GPL and are not
licensed for reuse. Use them to refer to this project, not to label your own.
